# Consolidated Decisions

### Release Contract

- The Release Contract is the single authoritative specification of a valid Application Release.
- Every Release shall conform to exactly one Release Contract version.
- The Platform shall consume Releases through the Contract rather than through application-specific assumptions.
- Application-specific behavior shall be declared through structured metadata rather than Platform logic.

---

### Packaging Philosophy

- Release generation is a hybrid engineering process.
- Claude may assist with extracting application knowledge.
- All Release artifacts shall ultimately be written by deterministic software.
- The final Release shall be completely deterministic regardless of how engineering knowledge was obtained.

---

### Separation of Responsibilities

- The Packaging Software creates Releases.
- The Validator evaluates Releases.
- The Platform deploys Releases.
- No component shall perform another component's responsibility.

---

### Sources of Truth

- The Release Manifest is the source of truth for an immutable Release.
- The Platform Operational Registry is the source of truth for deployment history.
- Host verification is the source of truth for the actual running system.
- These three sources shall never replace one another.

---

### Data Representation

- YAML represents immutable Release declarations.
- PostgreSQL stores Platform operational state.
- JSON Schema defines Manifest validity.
- Markdown is documentation only.
- Human-readable documentation shall never become the authoritative machine representation.

---

### Determinism

- Every engineering decision that affects deployment shall eventually become structured data.
- LLM reasoning shall not become part of the deployed Release.
- Validation results shall be reproducible.

---

### Evolution

- Releases are immutable.
- Contracts evolve through explicit versioning.
- Historical Releases remain interpreted by their declared Contract version.
- New tooling shall not silently reinterpret older Releases.

---

### Platform Philosophy

- The Platform consumes Releases.
- The Platform never repairs Releases.
- The Platform never generates engineering artifacts.
- The Platform records operational history without modifying Release history.
